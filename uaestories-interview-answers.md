# UAE Stories — interview answers (draft)

**Can you share a defining moment from your early life that shaped your career path?**

There wasn't one moment — there was a feeling I carried since childhood: that I was different and that I would go far, I just didn't know in which direction. For years I jumped from one thing to another. The defining moment came late, after enough mistakes to recognise the pattern: every time I was fully absorbed, it was markets, data, and figuring out why price moves. Once I saw that, the direction was obvious.

**What inspired you to enter your current industry, and what makes it the right fit for you?**

Trading is the most honest feedback loop I know. The market doesn't care about your story — it tells you within hours whether your reasoning was right. That suits me. I'm obsessive about understanding *why*, not just *what*, and I'd rather build the tool than trust someone else's black box. Six books, years of screen time, and a terminal I built from scratch are all the same instinct: don't guess, measure.

**What has been your biggest achievement so far, and why does it matter to you?**

Building TVC Fusion — a trading terminal that fuses on-chain data, technical structure, live order flow, institutional positioning, options pricing and macro into one decision layer, and then keeps itself honest with a paper-trading engine that audits every signal. It matters because it's proof of a path I chose against everyone's advice. The day it flagged a 2.5% flush an hour before a jobs report hit was the day I knew the years weren't wasted.

**What are you currently working on, and how does it align with your long-term goals?**

Making the system self-correcting: every rule is tested against real trades, win rates are measured per signal type, and anything below the bar gets switched off. I'm adding real-time spot-versus-leverage flow, whale positioning and a "fragility" gauge that warns before violent moves. Long term, I want a framework people can trust because it shows its work — not because someone with a big following says so.

**What is the most valuable lesson you've learned in your career, and how has it influenced your approach to work?**

That a beautiful thesis is worthless if the numbers say otherwise. I audited fifty of my own automated trades and found the strategy I was proudest of had a 3% win rate. Painful, but it changed everything: now nothing goes live without a measured track record, and every failure becomes a rule. Ego out, data in.

**What impact do you hope to leave in your industry, and how would you like to be remembered?**

Crypto is full of noise, predictions and people selling certainty. I want to be remembered for the opposite — transparency. Tools that show every input, every assumption, every loss. If people learn to ask "where's the data?" before they ask "what's the target?", I'll have done my job. And as someone who found her path late, stubbornly, and never let go of it.

**What advice would you give to your younger self at the start of your career, knowing what you know now?**

Stop apologising for being different — it's the whole advantage. The years of jumping between things weren't wasted; they were the research phase. Start measuring earlier: write down every decision and why, then check it against reality. And trust the pull you feel toward the thing you can't stop thinking about, even when nobody around you understands it yet.
